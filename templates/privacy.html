Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the service.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely to:

  • Provide and operate the Spendly expense tracking service.
  • Display your spending history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • Send essential account-related communications (e.g., password reset emails).
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues affecting the service.

We will never use your financial data to serve advertisements or sell insights to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We apply industry-standard measures including encrypted storage and secure connections (HTTPS) to protect your information.

You may delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, your personal data will be removed from our active systems within 30 days. Anonymised, aggregated data not linked to your identity may be retained for analytical purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database services) to operate the platform. These providers are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and only as directed by us.

We do not integrate with advertising networks, data brokers, or social media tracking tools.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Export your expense data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please use the options available in your account settings or contact us directly.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided in your account settings. We take privacy concerns seriously and will respond promptly.
